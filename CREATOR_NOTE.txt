Welcome to **CAM-ASCII-cpp**!

💡 **Where did it all begin?**

Around mid-September 2026, I started noticing new ASCII-graphics games making their way into the gaming scene. It got me thinking...

*What if ordinary webcam footage could be transformed into living ASCII art?*

Not just a few characters on a screen, but something that moves, reacts, changes, and brings that old-school text-art feeling to life — right through a camera!

And just like that, an idea began to take shape.

🛠️ **The journey began — 30 September 2026**

On **30/09/2026**, I started building this project. What began as an idea turned into an experiment with code, visuals, effects, and a whole lot of learning.

There were things to figure out, things to improve, and new ideas waiting around every corner. The goal wasn't just to make it work — it was to see how far I could take it.
